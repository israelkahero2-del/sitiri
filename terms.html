What Sitiri does

Sitiri replaces names and other identifying details in a document with placeholders, and puts them back later using a key that you save. It is a tool to help you share less. It is offered by [YOUR NAME].

Privacy

Sitiri runs inside your browser, on your own computer.

  • Your documents, your pasted text and your keys are not sent to us or to anyone else. We never receive, see or store them.
  • Sitiri has no accounts, no advertising and no visitor tracking.
  • Once Sitiri has loaded, it works with the internet switched off.
  • The company that hosts this website may record technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, as any web host does. Sitiri itself collects nothing.

It can miss things

  • Sitiri does not promise to find every identifying detail. Names without a title, place names, amounts and text inside pictures are easy to miss.
  • When Sitiri reads a scan or a photo, it can misread words and numbers. A misread name will not be hidden. You must compare the text with the page before you go on.
  • You are responsible for reading the preview and checking the redacted document before you share it.
  • A redacted document is not anonymous. Its facts may still point to the people or organizations involved, and anyone holding the key can reverse the placeholders.
  • Using Sitiri does not, by itself, make your use of an AI service or any other service lawful or compliant. That depends on your own obligations.
  • Nothing on this site is legal advice.

Your key

  • Keep the key private and separate from the redacted document.
  • If you lose the key, Sitiri cannot restore the document. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.
