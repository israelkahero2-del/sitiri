How it works

  1. 1

    Redact on your computer

    Choose a Word document, a PDF or a scan. Sitiri lists the names, addresses and numbers it finds. You add anything it missed.

  2. 2

    Share the redacted copy

    Send the copy with placeholders for review. Save your key and keep it to yourself.

  3. 3

    Restore with your key

    Load the key and the reviewed document. Sitiri puts every name back, with comments and tracked changes intact.
